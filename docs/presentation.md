# 6. Presentation and video

**Lead:** Petar Djordjevic

The team presents the project with a slide deck and a seven-minute video. The slides are also part of the project's documentation: they summarize the work, show the results, and link to the data, code, and app.

## Slides

The slides are a working draft and will be updated as the team fills them in.

## Outline

1. **Title:** project title and the full names of all team members
2. **Background:** Sandoval County's water resources and the concern about groundwater near Cuba
3. **Research questions:** what the project set out to answer
4. **Data:** the datasets used and where they come from
5. **Methods:** how the data were cleaned and analyzed
6. **Results:** charts, maps, and screenshots from the Streamlit app
7. **Findings:** what the data show, what they suggest, and where they are too limited to tell
8. **Limitations:** data gaps and what the results can't support
9. **Links:** the datasets, GitHub repository, and Streamlit app
