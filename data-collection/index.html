1. Data collection

Lead: Dylan

This stage covers finding public datasets that can answer the research questions, downloading them, and recording where each one comes from.

Datasets in the repository

water_flow_data_sandoval_county_sources.csv

USGS monthly and yearly statistics for 30 monitoring sites in Sandoval County (17 stream gages, 9 wells, 2 canals, and 2 reservoirs), with records from 1926 to 2026. Covers streamflow, depth to groundwater, reservoir storage, suspended sediment, water temperature, and water-quality measures: specific conductance, dissolved oxygen, pH, and turbidity.

ose_aquifer_test_wells.csv

New Mexico Office of the State Engineer aquifer-test records, 3,455 statewide and 179 in Sandoval County, including well depth, screened interval, geologic formation, and hydraulic properties such as transmissivity and storativity.

Still to collect

The proposal also calls for the following data, which isn't in the repository yet:

  • Aquifer geometry near Cuba: USGS San Juan Basin digital aquifer data for the Ojo Alamo Sandstone and San Jose Formation.
  • Water quality and salinity: the NM Bureau of Geology San Juan Basin Brackish Water Assessment and New Mexico Water Data records.
  • Acequias: locations and service areas of acequias and community ditches from the NM Interstate Stream Commission.
  • More water-level records: National Ground-Water Monitoring Network and Healy Collaborative Groundwater Monitoring Network data, to add wells beyond the 9 in the USGS file.
  • Shallow groundwater depth: the mean annual depth to groundwater for the shallow riparian aquifer dataset.
  • County boundary: a Sandoval County boundary layer to organize the maps.
